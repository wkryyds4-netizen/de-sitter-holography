The raw moment estimate is $Q_B\simeq3.17557649\,10^{-5}$.
Holding $L=12$, the initial nodes, and the series settings fixed,
mass-node refinement from four to six changes $D_B(2)$ by
$2.26752\,10^{-8}$ and the full Bose overlap by at most
$1.64309\,10^{-8}$ over these times. The coarser-to-finer comparison
is empirical. In particular it exceeds the analytic mass-tail bound;
that bound is not a total error bar.

All raw grids have been replayed from the unsimplified local vertices
$E$, inverse norms $W$, and density $\rho$, rather than the simplified
$\widehat R$ expression. The largest replay difference is below
$3\,10^{-17}$. The final integrated absolute flip-Hermiticity residual
is $6.78\,10^{-18}$ or smaller; this is a diagnostic, not a bound on
real symmetric numerical error. Six selected large weighted-condition
proxies have 55-digit reference comparisons, with maximum observed
relative error about $7.16\,10^{-11}$. Neither sample selection nor
condition numbers certify all remaining nodes.
