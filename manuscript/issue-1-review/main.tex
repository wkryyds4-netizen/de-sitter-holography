\section{What was inspected and what it establishes}

was opened on 6 November 2026 and targets commit
\href{https://github.com/wkryyds4-netizen/de-sitter-holography/commit/714d038bb605cfcffdc351e4dcc62aa95237d25f}{\texttt{714d038}}.
Its eight-page attachment and evidence ZIP were downloaded from the
public issue, saved unchanged, and read locally. All 43 entries in

match the local files. The attached PDF also matches its ZIP copy.
No supplied script was executed.

The original paper explicitly uses ordinary joint spectral calculus
and states Proposition 6.15 for Weyl relations \cite{KS}. In its
positive-curvature proof before (74), it asserts a cross strong
commutation; after (75), it uses fixed-vector analytic continuation.
These are the locations identified in the issue. They match the
project's earlier flip-domain, weak-continuation and representation
analyses. The external report therefore reinforces an existing
objection; it does not concern the current Susskind memory calculation.

For the precise realization, use $\hbar=1$ and
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal H=L^2(\mathbb R^2,dx\,dy/(2\pi^2)),\quad
 P_x=-\pi i\partial_x,\quad P_y=-\pi i\partial_y.
\end{equation}
The assignment $(\mathbf x,\mathbf y,\mathbf x',\mathbf y')=(X,Y,P_y,P_x)$
satisfies the regular Weyl hypotheses. With the paired function
$F=F_1^1$ of the paper, put
\begin{equation}
 A=F(X,Y),\quad B=F(P_y,P_x),\quad M=e^{iXY/\pi},\quad
 D=AB-BM^{-1}BMA.
\end{equation}
Differentiation on a core gives $M^{-1}P_yM=P_y+X$ and
$M^{-1}P_xM=P_x+Y$; joint spectral calculus then identifies the
sum factor with $M^{-1}BM$. The test vector
$f=\sqrt2\exp[-(x^2+y^2)/(2\pi)]$ has norm one.
All factors in $D$ are bounded unitaries. Intermediate chiral
domains cannot exclude $f$ from this final bounded identity.

\section{Analytic assessment}
For $\tau>0$ consider the normal operators
$N=X-i\tau Y$ and $T=P_y+i\tau P_x$. Their formal commutator
vanishes on the Schwartz core, whereas $[N,T^*]=2\pi\tau I$.
The decisive bounded witness follows from the Weyl relation:
\begin{equation}
 e^{iX}e^{iP_x}=-e^{iP_x}e^{iX},\qquad
 \|[e^{iX},e^{iP_x}]u\|=2\|u\|.
\end{equation}
Since these unitaries are bounded Borel functions of $N$ and $T$,
the claimed cross strong commutation is false in this realization.
This is an analytic conclusion independent of the interval certificate.
It does not by itself disprove every identity for particular chiral
functions on a specially chosen domain.

The first pole $z_0(h)=-\pi i(1+h)$, with $h=a+ib$, $b>0$, has
real spectral preimage
\begin{equation}
 x_h=\frac{\pi(a^2+b^2+a)}b,\qquad
 y_h=-\frac{\pi(1+a)}b.
\end{equation}
At $h=i$ this is $(\pi,-\pi)$, with residue $-1$ in the compact
product normalization. A Gaussian nonzero there is outside the
single chiral multiplication domain: its squared norm contains
a nonzero multiple of $\int_0^\epsilon dr/r$.
The pole map has positive Jacobian
$\pi^2((a+1)^2+b^2)/b^3$, so the fixed-domain obstruction persists
over an open parameter set. This supports the issue's objection
to literal fixed-vector holomorphy. A different contour prescription
would require an endpoint identification with the stated spectral
operator. The coefficient in (75) also needs the factor $i$:
$e^{\pi i h}$ becomes $e^{-\pi\tau}$ at $h=i\tau$.

\section{Independent endpoint recomposition}
The local driver \texttt{review\_issue1\_evidence.py} reads the ZIP
as data. Each binary64 Hermite coefficient is lifted to its exact
dyadic rational. Norms and inner products are then summed in
\texttt{Fraction} arithmetic. For positive rational $q$, use
\begin{equation}
 k=\left\lfloor\sqrt{\lfloor2^{640}q\rfloor}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad 2^{-320}k\leq\sqrt q\leq2^{-320}(k+1).
\end{equation}
The integer square root and rational inequalities certify these
enclosures without using the supplied assembly code or Arb.

For each of the five nonlinear overlaps we recompute
\begin{equation}
 e_j^2\leq\|v_j\|^2+\|w_j\|^2
 -2\big(I_{j,\mathrm{low}}-E_{j,\mathrm{up}}
             -T_{j,\mathrm{up}}-P_{j,\mathrm{up}}\big),
\end{equation}
using the saved integral, cubature-error, spatial-tail and product-tail
endpoints. The two chirp bounds use their saved overlap endpoints.
All seven saved upper bounds cover the corresponding independently
reconstructed squared-error upper values. We nevertheless use our
own square-root upper bounds for the final recomposition.

Writing $l,r$ for the terminal trial vectors and $E$ for the seven
step bounds, the independently checked inequalities are
\begin{align}
 \|Df\|&\geq\|l-r\|-E>0.04489650872,\\
 \inf_\theta\|ABf-e^{i\theta}BM^{-1}BMAf\|
 &\geq\sqrt{\|l\|^2+\|r\|^2-2|\langle r,l\rangle|}-E
 >0.04489650465.
\end{align}
These conclusions are conditional on the supplied overlap enclosures
and their analytic error estimates being correct. The local calculation
verifies the final rational assembly, not fresh integration of every
cell, and is not formal verification of the numerical libraries.
The original local certificate gave a margin approximately
$0.04489650872660254$; the external recomposition agrees at the
reported conservative threshold.
