\begin{abstract}
We complete the central-sector transfer of the actual neutral memory
component to local marked Wick descendants. A fixed-degree graph
interpolation estimate shows that the covariance filter on the actual
Jacobi correction agrees asymptotically with its Gaussian-chaos
multiplier. Together with the preceding covariance tangent and uniform
tails, this yields strong convergence on compact real-time intervals
at every fixed finite Schatten order. At half filling, the previously
established central source identity transfers the residual spectral
argument and exact energy pinching. The resulting finite-time moments
extend to controlled polynomial preparations and the single-selected
functional, retaining its local-size boundary terms.
\end{abstract}
\section{Model, object and scope}
Use standard CAR and the overlapping complex Gaussian p-body Hamiltonian
with the same static couplings in all sectors. Let
$\mathcal H_m=\wedge^m\mathbb C^N$,
$\norm{X}_{r,m}=(\E\tr_m|X|^r)^{1/r}$ and
$\tr_m=\binom Nm^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_m}$.
Take $\lambda_N=p^2/N\to\lambda>0$, fixed energy normalization,
$\hbar=1$, and string time $t$. Set
\[
 C_mI=\E H_m^2,\quad \Psi_m=\Phi_m/C_m,\quad
 \Phi_m(X)=\E_{H'}H'_mXH'_m ,
\]
where the independent copy only defines the deterministic covariance
map: every actual observable still uses the original disorder.
The exact neutral component is
\begin{equation}\label{Bactual}
 B_{m,N}(t)=m\epsilon_m(1-\Pin)e^{-iH_mt/2}
       \Phi_m(e^{i\alpha_mH_mt})e^{-iH_mt/2},
 \quad \alpha_m=1-p/m,\quad\epsilon_m=1-\alpha_m^2 .
\end{equation}
We work on $|m-N/2|\le L_0p$, so $m>p$ eventually.
$\Pin X=\sum_eP_eXP_e$ includes whole degenerate energy blocks.
Strong approximation below holds on this compact central window.
The limiting selected-state statements are at $m=N/2$.
The actual total memory field and its bilocal return still require
their separate sector interfaces.

\section{The missing composite filter estimate}
Let $\Ncal$ count coupling Gaussian chaos and $\Rcal$ be the output
$U(N)$ rank. On the equivariant Gaussian-matrix Hilbert space,
\[
 \Def=p\Ncal-\Rcal\ge0,\qquad
 \Psi_m=\mu_m(\Rcal),\qquad
 \theta_{j,m}=\mu_m(jp),\quad \Theta_m=\theta_m(\Ncal).
\]
The positive-part extension is
\[
 \mu_m(x)=\prod_{a=0}^{p-1}
 \left(1-\frac{x}{m-a}\right)_+
 \left(1-\frac{x}{N-m+p-a}\right)_+ .
\]
On a broad central interval it is Lipschitz with
$\operatorname{Lip}(\mu_m)\le12p/N$. These are the existing
fixed-sector rank and covariance identities \cite{rank,series}.
Define the finite-parameter Jacobi polynomials and their actual error:
\begin{align}
 a_j&=\sum_{v<j}\theta_{v,m},\quad Q_0=1,\quad Q_1=x,\quad
 Q_{j+1}=xQ_j-C_ma_jQ_{j-1},\nonumber\\
 Z_j&=\Wick_j(H^j),\quad E_j=Z_j-Q_j(H),\quad\eta_j=pE_j .
\end{align}
\begin{lemma}\label{filter}
For each fixed $j,L_0$,
\[
 \norm{(\Psi_m-\Theta_m)\eta_j}_{2,m}
 \le12\lambda_N
       \bigl(\norm{E_j}_{2,m}\norm{\Def^2E_j}_{2,m}\bigr)^{1/2}
 =O_{j,L_0}(p^{-1/2}).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The joint rank/chaos spectral decomposition gives
$\norm{(\Psi_m-\Theta_m)X}_2\le(12p/N)\norm{\Def X}_2$.
Spectral Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$\norm{\Def E}_2^2\le\norm E_2\norm{\Def^2E}_2$.
Insert $\eta=pE$ and $p^2/N=\lambda_N$.
The needed inputs, $\norm{E_j}_2=O(p^{-1})$ and
$\norm{\Def^2E_j}_2=O_j(1)$, are sectorwise; their audit follows.
\end{proof}
The first bound also follows directly from the preceding actual
$\eta_j$ envelope \cite{gradient,operator}. For the second, the Racah
sixth-moment certificate \cite{central,series} is polynomial in
$a/\sqrt N,r/\sqrt N,(m-N/2)/\sqrt N$. Its denominators stay nonzero
on compact sets, so it controls each fixed rank step with
$a\le p,r\le jp$ on the present window. It bounds both the fourth
deficit moment of the independent product chain and the fourth
one-step moment.

Gaussian creation symmetrization contracts normalized sector $L^2$.
The chain bound thus gives $\norm{\Def^2Z_j}_2=O_j(1)$.
For left multiplication $\mathcal T=H_{\rm left}$, the one-step bound
gives bounded $[\Def,[\Def,\mathcal T]]$ on each fixed-degree space.
The annihilation block is its adjoint. Iterating
$\Def^2\mathcal T=\mathcal T\Def^2+
2[\Def,\mathcal T]\Def+[\Def,[\Def,\mathcal T]]$
gives $\norm{\Def^2H^n}_2=O_n(1)$, hence the same bound for $Q_j(H)$.
No full-trace averaging is used.

\section{From the tangent to every fixed neutral coefficient}
On full neutral block families set
\[
 D=Q-\sum_i c_i^\dagger(\,\cdot\,)c_i,\qquad
 \bar D=N-Q-\sum_i c_i(\,\cdot\,)c_i^\dagger,\quad
 L=(D+\bar D)/2,\quad S_v=(vp-L)Z_v .
\]
Apply these maps before sector restriction. With target-sector
scalars $C_m,\theta_{v,m}$ retained, the preceding tangent \cite{tangent}
is
\begin{equation}\label{tangent}
 A_v=p(\Phi_m-C_m\theta_{v,m})Z_v,\qquad
 \norm{A_v-4\lambda_NC_m\theta_{v,m}S_v}_{r,m}=O_{v,r,L_0}(p^{-1}).
\end{equation}
The ordered coupling-Wick product is denoted by $\diamond$.
Define the local forced recurrence
\begin{align}
 \widehat r_j^S&=4\lambda_NC_m\sum_{v<j}\theta_{v,m}
                          (S_v\diamond Z_{j-1-v}),\nonumber\\
 \eh_{j+1}^S&=H\eh_j^S-C_ma_j\eh_{j-1}^S-\widehat r_j^S,
 \qquad\eh_0^S=\eh_1^S=\eh_2^S=0 .\label{etarec}
\end{align}
The exact actual recurrence has the same form with forcing
$\sum_{v<j}A_v\diamond Z_{j-1-v}$ \cite{series,operator}.
For a pure degree-$v$ error $X_v$,
\[
 X_v\diamond Z_w=\Wick_{v+w}(X_vH^w),\qquad
 \norm{\Wick_dY}_r\le2^d\norm Y_r\quad(\deg_{\rm Gaussian}Y\le d).
\]
Fixed-degree H\"older and \eqref{tangent}, followed by the finite
recurrence, prove
\begin{equation}\label{etaerror}
 \norm{\eta_j-\eh_j^S}_{r,m}=O_{j,r,L_0}(p^{-1})
 \quad(j,r\text{ fixed}).
\end{equation}
This estimate does not assert a degree-uniform error constant.

The actual and local neutral coefficients are
\begin{align}
 T_j&=A_j+(C_m\theta_{j,m}-\Phi_m)\eta_j,\nonumber\\
 V_j^S&=4\lambda_N\theta_{j,m}S_j+
                    (\theta_{j,m}-\Theta_m)\eh_j^S .\label{TV}
\end{align}
Their difference is exactly
\begin{align}
 C_m^{-1}T_j-V_j^S
 ={}&C_m^{-1}A_j-4\lambda_N\theta_{j,m}S_j\nonumber\\
 &+(\theta_{j,m}-\Theta_m)(\eta_j-\eh_j^S)
       -(\Psi_m-\Theta_m)\eta_j .\label{difference}
\end{align}
Here $\Theta_m$ is an $L^2$ contraction and $C_m$ is bounded above
and away from zero on the window. Lemma~\ref{filter} and
\eqref{etaerror} give $O_j(p^{-1/2})$ in $L^2$.
Both sides have bounded higher fixed Schatten norms. Interpolation
therefore gives $\norm{T_j-C_mV_j^S}_{r,m}\to0$ for every fixed
finite $r$. We do not claim the same rate at all $r$.

\section{The actual infinite series, before energy pinching}
The finite Jacobi coefficients satisfy the exact entire expansion
and the uniform estimate \cite{series,operator}
\[
 e^{itH}=\sum_{j\ge0}c_{j,m}(t)Q_j(H),\qquad
 |c_{j,m}(t)|\le e^{2\sqrt{AC_*}S}(2\sqrt A S)^j/j!,
 \quad |t|\le S ,
\]
where $a_j\le A$ and $C_m\le C_*$. Removing the samplewise conserved
function $\sum_jc_j\theta_jQ_j(H)$ gives the exact identity
\[
 p(1-\Pin)\Phi_m(e^{itH})
       =(1-\Pin)\sum_{j\ge0}c_{j,m}(t)T_j .
\]
The actual $A_j,\eta_j$ envelopes and CP contraction of $\Phi_m/C_m$
give, on the window and at fixed $r$,
\begin{equation}\label{tails}
 \norm{T_j}_{r,m}+\norm{V_j^S}_{r,m}
       \le K_r(j+1)^{b_r}L_r^j\sqrt{j!}.
\end{equation}
For the local term this follows from the pure $S_v$ envelope, the
OU/Chebyshev projection bound and the fully expanded recurrence
\eqref{etarec}. Expand that recurrence before H\"older: its two
product layers require only a fixed multiple of $r$, not an exponent
doubling at every degree. The same argument controls
$\Theta_m\eh_j^S$ by an exponential-in-$j$ factor, absorbed in $L_r$.
All these bounds are already sectorwise in \cite{operator,gradient}.

Define the finite local proxy
\[
 \widetilde B^S_{m,N}(t)=
 \frac{m\epsilon_mC_m}{p}(1-\Pin)e^{-iH_mt/2}
       \sum_{j\ge0}c_{j,m}(\alpha_mt)V_j^S e^{-iH_mt/2}.
\]
\begin{theorem}\label{strong}
For every fixed finite $r,S,L_0$,
\[
 \sup_{|m-N/2|\le L_0p}\sup_{|t|\le S}
 \norm{B_{m,N}(t)-\widetilde B^S_{m,N}(t)}_{r,m}\longrightarrow0 .
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The tail of either series after $J$ is bounded by a constant times
\[
 \sum_{j>J}(j+1)^{b_r}L_{r,S}^j/\sqrt{j!}\longrightarrow0
\]
uniformly in $N,m,t$. The external phases are unitary,
$m\epsilon_m/p\le2$, and $\norm{1-\Pin}_{r\to r}\le2$.
At fixed $J$, apply \eqref{difference} term by term. First take
$N\to\infty$, then $J\to\infty$.
\end{proof}
The approximation uses the original common disorder and retains the
finite-$N$ energy projection. It uses the rank graph bounds only on
fixed-degree errors; no high-Schatten bound on a nonlinear rank
multiplier or rank-marked proxy is assumed.

\section{Central limiting fields and exact removal of static mass}
Now set $m=N/2$, $C_m\to C>0$, $q=e^{-4\lambda}\in(0,1)$.
Each term in \eqref{etarec} is a finite linear combination of
\[
 \Wick_d[H^a(S_v\diamond Z_w)] .
\]
Its L mark lies in a pure Wick subblock; any exterior H slots and
overall chaos filter are allowed. The central all-G source identity
of \cite{higher} therefore applies to arbitrary products of these
primitives and their adjoints. Its local exponent agrees with the
earlier tracial exponent, directly in the central sector. Thus the
regression and two-energy proof of \cite{desc} transfers without
inferring a sector law from a full-charge average.

Let $H_j^{(q,C)}$ be monic q-Hermite, $P_j(q)=[j]_q!$, and
\[
 \kappa_j=\lambda\frac{(j^2-4q\partial_q)P_j}{P_j}.
\]
For $g_j=\operatorname{Reg}\eh_j^S$, the existing regression is
\begin{align*}
 g_{j+1}(x)&=xg_j(x)-C[j]_qg_{j-1}(x)
 -4\lambda C\left(\sum_{v<j}q^v\kappa_v\right)H_{j-1}^{(q,C)}(x),\\
 g_0&=g_1=g_2=0,\qquad g_j=\sum_dg_{jd}H_d^{(q,C)} .
\end{align*}
The regression of $V_j=4\lambda q^jS_j+(q^j-q^\Ncal)\eh_j^S$ is
\[
 v_j(x)=4\lambda q^j\kappa_jH_j^{(q,C)}(x)
           +\sum_{d\le j-2}(q^j-q^d)g_{jd}H_d^{(q,C)}(x).
\]
Define $N_j=V_j-v_j(H)$. For finite $N$, use the same polynomial
$v_{j,N}$ at $(\lambda_N,q_N,C_m)$ and set
$N_{j,N}=V_{j,N}^S-v_{j,N}(H_m)$.

In the two-energy cut-diagram expansion, subtracting $v_j$ cancels
all groups with no direct field bridge. If $D$ bounds the two fields'
raw length, the remaining normalized energy-Hermite coefficients obey
\[
 |M_{ab}|\le K_D(a+b+1)^D
 q^{\max(0,\min(a,b)-D)}\,\mathbf1_{|a-b|\le D}.
\]
This square-summable bound gives an $L^2(\rho_{q,C}\otimes\rho_{q,C})$
spectral density, hence zero mass on the energy diagonal.
It is the old fixed-degree proof \cite{desc}, now with central moments.

The finite positive measure
$\nu_N(A,B)=\E\tr_m[N_{j,N}^\dagger P_H(B)N_{j,N}P_H(A)]$
has this weak limit. Higher central moments give tightness and uniform
integrability; the compact limiting moment problem identifies it.
Portmanteau on the closed diagonal yields
\[
 \norm{\Pin N_{j,N}}_{2,m}^2=\nu_N(\{E=E'\})\longrightarrow0.
\]
Higher fixed moments and pinching contraction upgrade this to all
finite $r$. Since $v_{j,N}(H)$ commutes exactly with $H$,
$(1-\Pin)V_{j,N}^S-N_{j,N}=-\Pin N_{j,N}\to0$.

\section{Finite-time selected moments}
The limiting neutral field is the convergent noncommutative $L^r$
series
\begin{equation}\label{limitfield}
 B_\infty(t)=2C e^{-iHt/2}
       \sum_{j\ge1}c_j^{(q,C)}(t)N_j e^{-iHt/2}.
\end{equation}
To justify every fixed finite $r$, first apply the preceding fixed-$j$
pinching result. The norm of the limiting $N_j$ is bounded by the
limiting norm of $(1-\Pin)V_{j,N}^S$, hence by twice \eqref{tails}.
This supplies the limiting series tail, without assuming a uniform
high-norm bound on the entire regression map. The finite Jacobi
coefficients converge on compact times; keep $\alpha_m$ and
$m\epsilon_m/p$ until this fixed-truncation limit.

H\"older and telescoping transfer all fixed mixed covariant words
in $B(t)$, its adjoint, fixed energy polynomials/phases, and the already
controlled charged time fields. Times range over fixed compact sets.
For a controlled fixed polynomial preparation $P_NO$,
\[
 \E\norm{X_NP_NO}^2\le C_P\norm{X_N}_{4,m}^2
\]
by the finite-$N$ orbit-density theorem \cite{prep}.
Together with polarization this transfers errors inside
$\E\langle O|P_N^\dagger\mathcal B_NQ_N|O\rangle$.
Preparation degree stays fixed. Multiple arbitrary nonpolynomial
selected factors interlaced with separate pinched blocks are not
included merely by this preparation argument.

For one normalized selected excitation, put
$h=H/\sqrt C$, $W=(S_1-\lambda H)/\sqrt C$, $s=\sqrt q$.
The full boundary functional \cite{higher} extends through the
convergent tails:
\begin{equation}\label{density}
 \sigma_1(\mathcal B)=
 [1-s(1+\lambda)]\tau(\mathcal B)
 +s\tau\bigl((h-W)\mathcal B(h-W)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Use the actual phases in \eqref{limitfield}. This functional need not
be stationary on the full marked algebra. The charged-only expression
$\tau((1-s+sh^2)\mathcal B)$ is not a replacement for \eqref{density}
on neutral or mixed words.

As a calibration, $N_1=4\lambda q(S_1-\lambda H)=4\lambda q\sqrt C\,W$,
$\tau(W^2)=\lambda$, and
\[
 \sigma_1(N_1^2)=16\lambda^3Cq^2[1+\lambda s(1+q)].
\]
This recovers the earlier slope result in the complete-field interface.
In string time,
\[
 \sigma_1(B_\infty'(0)^\dagger B_\infty'(0))
       =64\lambda^3C^3q^2[1+\lambda\sqrt q(1+q)] .
\]
Using the charged-only density would omit the positive relative
correction $\lambda\sqrt q(1+q)$. Rational source checks recover this
difference exactly; they do not identify rational $(\gamma,\lambda)$
with the physical curve $\gamma=e^{-\lambda}$.

\section{Executed checks and the remaining return problem}
The new finite checker keeps all Gaussian coordinates in
$(N,p,m)=(3,1,2),(4,1,2),(4,2,2)$ through degrees $5,4,3$.
An independently constructed CAR Casimir diagonalizes the rank filter;
the covariance is also built directly from its Kraus matrices.
Fifteen degree rows check the graph Cauchy--Schwarz inequality and
the composite-filter estimate, with six nonzero filter rows.
The largest direct-versus-spectral Hermite error is below
$9.7\cdot10^{-16}$; these are floating-point consistency tests with
explicit tolerances, not interval certificates.

The rational local-source compiler retains pure-Wick regions, local
L subblocks, overall chaos and OU degree weights. At two independent
rational $(\gamma,\lambda)$ points it checks 16 two-energy moments
against the old descendant evaluator and 40 regression cancellations.
It also checks 16 selected boundary identities and two explicit
counterchecks to the charged-only density. No finite-$N$ energy
pinching simulation is used as evidence for its limiting theorem.

The graph inputs are inherited, not recalculated: fixed-sector
rank transition orthogonality, the compact-window sixth-moment
certificate, and fixed-degree multiplication bounds. The decisive
new combination is Lemma~\ref{filter} applied to the \emph{actual}
$pE_j$, followed by \eqref{difference}. This avoids reconstructing a
rank-proxy exponential just to resolve the composite filter.

All science outputs are preserved after their first successful run.
The evidence manifest records code versions, parameters, inherited
file hashes, the two-pass PDF and final rendered pages. No Git push
or issue update accompanies this local stage.
