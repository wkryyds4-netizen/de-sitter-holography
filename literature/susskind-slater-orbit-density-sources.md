# Sources and novelty boundary: Slater orbit density

Read 2026-11-08. Local starting evidence: `susskind-slater-neutral-slope` manuscript, Chinese note, latest journal and its sealed manifest. That stage left exact pinching in selected excited states open; its limiting density was not a finite-N bound.

- Joel A. Tropp, [Second-Order Matrix Concentration Inequalities](https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/Tro16-Second-Order-Matrix-preprint.pdf), §§6–7: Lemma 6.1 gives Gaussian integration by parts for matrix trace moments; Proposition 7.3 and the proof of Proposition 7.1 bound the mixed powers by the variance-weighted even moment. We use this standard argument with the exact covariance trace 2 d_m a_N to avoid an irrelevant ambient dimension factor. The general matrix-Gaussian inequality is established literature.
- The Slater orbit-frame bound is proved directly here using compact-group decomposition, Schur averaging, multiplicities and the Weyl orbit of the weight (1^m,0^(N−m)). It does not rely on a guessed asymptotic spectrum or a multiplicity-free assertion.
- The projective Haar moments in the finite checks follow by twirling |psi><psi| tensor powers onto the symmetric subspace. The manuscript gives the permutation formula through degree three; it is an elementary exact diagnostic, not a new integration theorem.

The new project-specific step is the finite-N bound on both variance operators of the orbit synthesis map for linear stabilizer-covariant preparations. Its application supplies uniform Lr densities and transfers the actual neutral-slope pinching error in the one-selected preparation. General nonlinear selected preparations still require a new argument.
