# Stationary states: sources and model interface

Read on 2026-11-08 before the new proof and computation. The previous transfer manuscript and its evidence manifest are the model input; the statements below are not a claim of priority for general operator-algebra facts.

- Jan Dereziński, [math-ph/0511030v2](https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0511030v2#S8.SS4), §§8.3–8.6, especially (8.64) and the paragraph following (8.65): normal states of B(H) are trace-class densities; invariance means commutation with the Hamiltonian; the Gibbs criterion uses the ordinary trace. This directly supplies the correct representation-dependent question. The manuscript gives its own short compact-eigenspace proof of absence of normal stationary states and separately treats singular states.
- Rajgadia–Xu, [2604.14387v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14387v3#S4.SS2), §4.2 (102)–(110), and §9.2: state-adapted dressed operators yield a Type I closure through a compact grading operator, while the authors distinguish their dynamical interpretation from generic probes. Their independent-matter model is not our same-J complex model. Their conclusion is a precedent for the algebra choice, not a proof of our stationary-state classification. We do not attribute our quotient formula to the paper.
- Rahman–Susskind, [2401.08555v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08555v3), §§1–2, §8.2, particularly (1.11), (2.23)–(2.29), (8.78): Boltzmann, Hawking and tomperature notions must be separated, including the distinction between cosmic and string units. Infinite Boltzmann temperature in cosmic units need not mean infinite temperature in string units. No numerical temperature is imported into the present dimensionless h convention.
- Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten, [2206.10780v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10780v3#S2.SS4), §2.4 (22)–(29): observer energy positivity selects a finite corner of a crossed product, with a normalized trace and a maximum-entropy state. This is an algebra-changing construction, not normalization of the ordinary B(H) trace in our representation. We use it only to distinguish the physical alternatives; no equivalence to our model is asserted.
